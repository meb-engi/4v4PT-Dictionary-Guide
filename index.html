Welcome to the ASIA + OCE 4v4 PASS Time Website!

This website was created to help new players of 4v4 PASS Time for both the Asian and Oceanian regions. Both regions are very close and players from either play on the other very often due to low ping differences.

What is 4v4 PASS Time?

4v4 PASS Time is a fast paced basketball-like gamemode where two teams of 4 fight for possession of the jack and use a combination of movement tech and teamwork to score goals.

Video from passtime.tf

It is very different from other TF2 competitive modes such as 6s and Highlander with its unique class and weapon restrictions, effectively being its own seperate thing. It's getting its own source mod, which you can learn more about at the PASS Fortress Website.

The Classes

There are three (3) classes you can play in 4v4 PASS Time, each having their own roles and playstyles. They are the Soldier, Demoman, and Medic.

Soldier

The most versatile of the three classes. Excels at both offense and defense, and is the most common class in the gamemode.

MAX PLAYERS PER TEAM: 3

Demoman

The powerhouse of the team. With your grenade launcher you deal the most damage in the whole game (Pipes), and with your booties you have the highest mobility at the cost of damage (Stoic).

MAX PLAYERS PER TEAM: 1

Medic

The sole healer in the entire game. Buffs teammates to be able to last longer, while being a nuisance to the enemy team with the fastest walk speed and furthest throw range.

MAX PLAYERS PER TEAM: 1
